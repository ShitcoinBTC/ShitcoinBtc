How it works

1. The seller lists an item with a USDC price. 2. The buyer pays with any token on the network. The escrow converts it to USDC on the spot. 3. The seller ships with USPS, UPS, or FedEx and marks the order shipped. 4. When the carrier confirms delivery, the escrow pays the seller in USDC minus the 2% fee. If anything goes wrong, either side can open a dispute before delivery and a human reviews it.
