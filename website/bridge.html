How it will work

UTXO → NEVM

You burn SHIT in a special UTXO transaction naming your NEVM address. The paired NEVM client sees the burn and the Vault Manager mints the same amount of SHIT on the contract layer. The burn is the deposit — no custodian ever touches it.

→

NEVM → UTXO

You freeze SHIT in the Vault Manager contract on NEVM. Anyone can then submit the SPV proof of that freeze to the UTXO chain, which mints your SHIT back on the payment layer. Every step is verifiable on-chain.

Trust model: SPV proofs plus the deterministic masternode quorum — as decentralized as the network itself. Vault Manager: 0x7904299b3D3dC1b03d1DdEb45E9fDF3576aCBd5f

Wrap: SHIT → wSHIT

Deposit native SHIT to the wrap vault, then claim wSHIT 1:1 on Base or Solana. The vault holds part of the on-chain 6% wrapping reserve — every movement is verifiable on the explorers.

  1. Send SHIT to the vault: sys1q9h2mfp4u0aer6k3t6pnx332g4cmf399dmhwuha (view on explorer). Wait for 6 confirmations (~3 min).
  2. Paste your deposit txid and your destination address below.
  3. The watcher verifies the deposit and sends wSHIT 1:1 from team inventory. Minimum 100 SHIT.

Unwrap: wSHIT → SHIT

Send wSHIT back to the team inventory address, then claim native SHIT 1:1 to any SHIT address. The vault releases your SHIT on-chain.

  1. Send your wSHIT to the team inventory address (shown on this page once the contract is live).
  2. Paste the return tx hash and the SHIT address you want paid.
  3. The watcher verifies the return and releases SHIT 1:1 from the vault.

Trust model: team-operated vault backed by the on-chain 6% SHIT wrapping reserve (paid in block 1). Every movement is verifiable — SHIT side on the Shitcoin explorer, Base on BaseScan, Solana on Solscan. This is the honest interim until the trustless UTXO↔NEVM peg ships with the NEVM upgrade.
