Shity Launchpad on TON

The TON launchpad is two Tact contracts: Sale and LaunchpadFactory. Creating a sale costs a flat 15 TON, sent automatically to the protocol wallet on every sale deployment — a quarter of the 60 TON the big pads charge. Deploying from your own wallet is next; the compiled contracts and instructions are below.

Sale contract

Send deploy_sale (text message) with 15.05 TON to the sale's computed address along with its state init: exactly 15 TON goes to the fee wallet immediately, 0.05 TON stays as the sale's operating reserve, anything above is refunded. Pay the fee before funding — an unpaid sale is an inert brick until the 15 TON lands.

Download Sale bytecode (.boc)

LaunchpadFactory (optional wrapper)

Send LaunchSale with 15.35 TON and the factory deploys a sale for you (you become the creator) and refunds your change.
