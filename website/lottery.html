How to play

  1. Send 10 SHIT per ticket to the lottery address below. Max 100 tickets per player per round.
  2. At the draw block, the winner is picked by block hash from all tickets. The draw only happens with at least 2 players; otherwise tickets roll over to the next round.
  3. Winner gets 80% of the pot, 20% goes to the team. Paid in SHIT.
Tickets are counted from confirmed payments to the lottery address.
