Shity Shield

Deep check any transaction before you sign it, or run a free quick scan on a token, link, or wallet. Deep checks cost a flat $1.

Honest framing: the Shield checks known scam patterns and decodes what your transaction does. A clean scan is not a guarantee. New drainers appear every day. Never sign anything you do not understand.

How it works

  1. Connect your wallet and pick a chain.
  2. Paste the transaction you are about to sign: the to address, the value, and the data.
  3. Pay the flat $1 fee. That is how the Shield stays alive.
  4. Get the verdict: what the transaction does, in plain words, plus every scam flag found.

Where do I find the transaction data?

In Rabby, open the signing popup and click the data tab. In MetaMask, click the transaction details dropdown on the confirmation screen. On most dapps you can also find it on the block explorer by looking up the contract you are interacting with. Copy the full hex starting with 0x.
